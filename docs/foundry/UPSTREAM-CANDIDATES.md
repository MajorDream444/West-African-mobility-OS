## The rule this document enforces

Field evidence and lessons flow **upward**. Authority and policy flow **downward**. These are different directions and must not be confused.

> Field experience cannot automatically modify universal doctrine. One experimental corridor decision must never silently become a universal rule binding every future industry.

Everything below is a **candidate**, not a contribution. Nothing on this list moves to [HAMAL_MOB_PLAYBOOKS](https://github.com/MajorDream444/HAMAL_MOB_PLAYBOOKS) until the corridor has actually tested it and a reviewed pull request carries it, with the corridor evidence attached.

**Do not copy any of this upstream word-for-word today.** First prove which parts are genuinely universal and which are artifacts of one automotive corridor in one region. A pattern that has run once has been observed, not validated.

## What must not travel upward at all

- **Corridor claims and evidence.** `CLM-` and `DEC-` records are mobility truth. HAMAL holds doctrine, not this corridor's facts.
- **Counterparty names, relationships, and provenance chains.**
- **Anything still classified `ASSUMPTION`, `OPEN`, or `CONFLICT`.** Unresolved corridor questions are not doctrine in any state.
- **Prototype output that has not passed a truth gate.**

## The proposal path

1. The corridor uses the pattern in real operation and records what happened, including failures.
2. The proving condition in the table above is met and evidenced.
3. A **generalised** version is drafted — corridor specifics stripped, not merely renamed.
4. A pull request to HAMAL_MOB_PLAYBOOKS carries the generalisation, the corridor evidence, and an explicit statement of what remained industry-specific.
5. HAMAL reviews and decides. **HAMAL's decision is authoritative**; the corridor conforms to whatever comes back down, including a rejection or a materially altered version.
6. The outcome is recorded in this repository's decision register.

A pattern rejected upstream stays in use here if it works here. That is not a conflict — it is the correct outcome of an industry implementation being more specific than universal doctrine.
