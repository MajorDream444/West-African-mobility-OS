## What Cohort 001 is for

Not scale. Not revenue. Not headlines.

Cohort 001 exists to answer six questions with evidence, so that Cohort 002 is designed from observation rather than assumption:

1. Can we recruit and honestly assess the people we say we can reach?
2. Does the curriculum produce capability that survives **external** assessment?
3. Do operating cells serve real customers at a quality level an institutional fleet would accept?
4. Does the HAMAL layer actually get used at the point of work, or does the crew revert to paper?
5. Does the founder lifecycle produce anyone genuinely ready for S5 — and how long did it really take?
6. What did we get wrong?

A cohort that produces good answers to these and zero ventures has succeeded. A cohort that produces three ventures and no reliable answers has not.

---

## Preconditions

Cohort 001 does not open until:

- [ ] Phase 0 definition of done is met (`AGENTS.md`)
- [ ] Workshop registry exists for the intake city — the registry is the placement infrastructure
- [ ] At least one operating cell with real paying customers exists for S2 placement
- [ ] HAMAL Wave 1 services are live: Tier 0, work orders, technician credentials
- [ ] Data-rights legal review complete in the intake country, or intake limited to minimum-consent evidence collection
- [ ] Curriculum written, and assessment rubrics published **before** recruitment opens
- [ ] Named instructors, named assessors, at least one assessor external to the programme
- [ ] Safety standards, insurance, and incident process in place
- [ ] Funding secured for the full cohort duration — a cohort abandoned mid-way costs participants their time and the corridor its credibility

**Recruiting before these exist is the single most likely way this programme harms the people it intends to serve.** A participant who leaves paid work for a cohort that collapses has paid a real price for our optimism.

## Composition targets

Targets are **published and monitored**, not quietly hoped for. `cohort-inclusion-monitor` tracks and publishes gaps, including failures.

| Group | Target | Structural change required |
|---|---|---|
| Women | ≥ 30% | Schedule, facilities, safe transport, women instructors, track 18 pathway |
| Participants under 25 | ≥ 40% | Apprenticeship-shaped entry; guardian consent where under majority |
| Existing working mechanics | ≥ 30% | Evening and rotating schedule; they cannot stop earning |
| Participants without a smartphone | Not excluded | Device provision or shared-device and offline capture workflow |
| Participants outside the capital | ≥ 15% | Transport, accommodation, or a satellite delivery model |
| Caregivers | Not excluded | Childcare provision or schedule accommodation |

**If a target is missed, the published finding names the structural cause, not the applicant pool.** "Women did not apply" is not a finding; it is an unexamined design failure.

## Twelve-month arc

### Months 0–1 · S0 Apply and assess
Outreach through workshops, technical schools, the registry, women's associations, and community networks. Practical assessment, not written examination in a second language. Consent captured at intake. Baseline capability recorded for every applicant, including those not admitted — with their consent, this is the corridor's first honest picture of the technician base.

### Months 1–4 · S1 Learn and certify
Curriculum blocks per `FOUNDER-LIFECYCLE.md` S1. Technical core, service operations, business core, digital core, professional core. Assessment by rubric with at least one external assessor. HAMAL competence demonstrated on live work-order entry, not simulated.

**Checkpoint:** external assessment pass rate. If it is low, the curriculum is wrong, not the participants.

### Months 4–9 · S2 Join operating team
Placement into operating cells with real paying customers. Every job logged in HAMAL at the point of work. Named supervision. Rework rate, conduct, and reliability recorded.

**Checkpoint:** is the tenant actually being used at the bay, or is data entered retrospectively from paper? This is the single most predictive signal about whether the platform is real.

### Months 9–12 · S3 Produce verified results
Defined individual responsibility. Four measured periods. First-time-fix, rework, cycle time, parts accuracy, evidence quality, honesty of reporting.

**Checkpoint:** how many participants reach the S3 bar, and how many reach the S4 gate? That ratio sizes Cohort 002 and the venture pipeline realistically.

### Continuous · Venture pipeline
Signals collected from participants throughout — a person doing the work sees the real problem. Strong signals enter V0. The first V1 thesis gates are expected around month 9, informed by the founder's actual S2–S3 record rather than by their pitch.

## What Cohort 001 measures about itself

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Applicants, admitted, completed, by group | Pipeline honesty and inclusion reality |
| External assessment pass rate | Curriculum quality without self-marking |
| Placement rate into operating cells | Whether the corridor can absorb its own graduates |
| Rework and first-time-fix by participant over time | Whether capability actually compounds |
| Work orders logged at the point of work vs. retrospectively | Whether the platform is used or performed |
| Participants reaching S3 / S4 bars | Realistic lifecycle throughput |
| Consent revocations and data-rights complaints | Whether the rights are real |
| Instructor development — local trainers certifying unaided | The core anti-extraction indicator |
| Drop-out reasons, recorded honestly | Where the design fails people |
| Cost per participant to each stage | Whether the model can be replicated |

All of it is reported with the same discipline and cadence as any success, including the bad numbers. Aggregate results publish subject to the privacy, safety, legal, security, and confidentiality controls in constitution §3.7; those controls protect people, and may not be used to conceal programme performance.

## Known risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Recruiting before operating cells exist — trained people with nowhere to go | Hard precondition above; placement capacity sizes the cohort, not ambition |
| Curriculum written for a Western workshop | Local instructors co-author; assessment against local vehicle parc and local tooling reality |
| Platform not used at the point of work | Offline capture, device provision, digital core as an assessed S1 credential |
| Participants forgoing income | Stipend question resolved before recruitment (`OPEN`) |
| Inclusion targets missed and rationalised | Published findings must name structural cause |
| Ownership expectations forming informally | Constitution §3.1 and ownership rules communicated at S0, in writing, in the participant's language |
| Cohort abandoned mid-way | Full-duration funding as a hard precondition |
| Success narrated ahead of evidence | Language gate on every external communication |

## Definition of done

- 20–30 participants assessed, admitted, and honestly recorded
- Credential outcomes verified by external assessment
- Participants placed in operating cells with logged, auditable work
- At least one participant demonstrably at the S4 bar
- At least one venture through G2 with real demand evidence
- Cohort 002 designed from **observed** figures, replacing the `ASSUMPTION` labels in this document
- Full results published, including failures
- Country replication package updated with what actually happened
