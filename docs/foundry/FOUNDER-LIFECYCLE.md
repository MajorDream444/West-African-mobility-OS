# Founder Lifecycle — S0 to S7

**Status:** `PROPOSAL`
**Parent:** [`MOBILITY-VENTURE-FOUNDRY.md`](../../MOBILITY-VENTURE-FOUNDRY.md) §4
**Machine-readable:** [`data/foundry/founder-ladder-rungs.csv`](../../data/foundry/founder-ladder-rungs.csv)

---

## The rule that governs every rung

> A participant advances when **evidence** says they advanced, recorded by a **named human**, against a **published bar** they could see before they started.

Time served does not promote. Enthusiasm does not promote. Being liked does not promote. Being the first person in the room does not promote.

This is the same ladder proposed for Diallo in `VISION.md` §6 (`candidate → envoy → operator → commercial leader → partner → builder and mentor`), generalised so it is reusable for every participant and every country lead rather than negotiated once per relationship.

---

## Stage summary

| Stage | Name | Typical duration | Economic status | Promotion authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S0** | Apply and assess | 1–3 weeks | None | Cohort admissions panel |
| **S1** | Learn and certify | 8–16 weeks | Stipend possible; no equity | Assessment board (2 assessors, 1 external) |
| **S2** | Join operating team | 3–6 months | Wage or contract | Operating-cell lead |
| **S3** | Produce verified results | 6–12 months | Wage + performance component | Cell lead + quality assurance |
| **S4** | Manage customers and assets | 9–18 months | Wage + margin share on managed book | Country operating lead |
| **S5** | Launch or acquire venture | Gate-driven | Founder economics; risk accepted | Venture gate panel (see stage gates) |
| **S6** | Earn ownership | Multi-year, vesting | Vested instrument per pathway | Governance body + executed instrument |
| **S7** | Train the next cohort | Ongoing | Instructor economics + legacy interest | Foundry standards board |

Durations are `ASSUMPTION` — planning figures for cohort design, not entitlements. A participant may hold at a stage indefinitely, exit and re-enter, or move laterally into a different venture track without demotion.

## S0 · Apply and assess

**Purpose:** establish a truthful baseline, not to filter for polish.

**Intake captures:** current skill (self-reported and demonstrated), languages (Arabic, French, English, Hassaniya, Wolof, Pulaar), tooling access, transport, caregiving and time constraints, prior business attempts including failures, literacy and numeracy, digital access (device, connectivity, data cost), and the participant's own stated goal.

**Assessment is practical.** A diagnostic bench task, a numeracy task grounded in parts pricing, and a structured conversation. Not a written exam in a second language.

**Exit evidence to advance:**
- Completed intake record with consent captured (`DATA-RIGHTS-POLICY.md` §2)
- Practical assessment scored by two assessors
- Baseline capability grade recorded
- Track preference recorded (may change later)

**Explicitly not created at S0:** any promise of placement, income, equity, or admission to a later stage.

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** admitting on charisma and discovering at S3 that the underlying capability was never measured.

---

## S1 · Learn and certify

**Purpose:** verifiable technical and commercial capability.

**Curriculum spine** (adapted per track):

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Technical core | Diagnostics, engine and driveline, electrical and auto-electric, hybrid and EV safety and systems, tooling discipline |
| Service operations | Work orders, intake and handover, warranty process, parts identification, first-time-fix discipline |
| Business core | Costing, pricing, cash cycle, customer records, basic accounts, tax and registration reality in-country |
| Digital core | HAMAL workspace, work-order entry, evidence capture, photo and VIN discipline, data-rights literacy |
| Professional core | Customer communication, safety, dispute handling, honest reporting of unknowns |

**Certification is verified, not attended.** Each credential requires a practical demonstration assessed against a published rubric, at least one assessor external to the participant's own instructor, and a dated record in the credential registry.

**Exit evidence to advance:** credential record with assessor identities and dates; safety clearance; HAMAL workspace competence demonstrated on live work-order entry.

**Portability:** the participant holds rights of access, correction, consent, and portability over their credential and assessment record, permanently, including after they leave — subject to applicable law and to legitimate safety, warranty, employment, and audit obligations (constitution §3.2, `DATA-RIGHTS-POLICY.md` §4). Servicing a venture's customer does not transfer that customer relationship to the participant; customer records are governed by §3.3.

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** certificate inflation — the single fastest way to destroy the corridor's credibility with an OEM, an institutional fleet, or an insurer.

---

## S2 · Join operating team

**Purpose:** real customers, real consequences, supervised.

The participant joins an **operating cell** — a small crew (typically 3–8) delivering actual service to actual paying customers under an existing venture or a foundry-run cell. Work is logged in HAMAL from day one. Supervision is explicit and named.

**Exit evidence to advance:**
- Minimum logged work orders completed to standard (cohort-specific; published before intake)
- Rework rate below the published threshold
- Customer-facing conduct record clean
- Attendance and reliability record
- No unresolved safety incident

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** graduating people into ownership who have never carried a real customer's failure.

---

## S3 · Produce verified results

**Purpose:** move from "does the work" to "owns the outcome."

The participant carries defined responsibility: a bay, a route, a vehicle class, a customer segment, a parts line. Performance is measured on the metrics the corridor actually competes on.

**Measured:** first-time-fix rate, rework, cycle time, parts accuracy, warranty claim quality, customer satisfaction, evidence quality (are work orders complete and photo-verified?), and honesty of reporting — a well-flagged unknown scores above a confident unsourced answer, exactly as in the field-evidence protocol.

**Exit evidence to advance:** four consecutive periods of metrics at or above standard, with the underlying records auditable; at least one documented instance of correctly escalating a problem beyond their own authority.

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** promoting the person with the best relationships rather than the best record.

---

## S4 · Manage customers and assets

**Purpose:** commercial responsibility — a book of business, a team, or an asset base.

The participant manages a customer book, an asset (a mobile unit, a bay, a fleet contract, a parts line), or a small team. They now carry P&L visibility, pricing discretion within bounds, and team development duty.

**Measured, in addition to S3:** revenue retention, margin discipline, receivables, asset uptime, team members developed to the next rung, dispute resolution, and governance behaviour (does this person follow the evidence rules when nobody is watching?).

**Exit evidence to advance:** managed book or asset performing to standard across a full cycle including at least one adverse period; at least one team member advanced a rung under their development; clean governance record.

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** creating founders who can execute but cannot hold a customer relationship or a cash cycle.

---

## S5 · Launch or acquire venture

**Purpose:** formation.

The participant now enters the venture pipeline as a founder or acquiring operator. Their S0–S4 record is a primary input to the `V1 Thesis` gate — the foundry is explicitly biased toward founders with a verified operating record over founders with a strong pitch.

Routes at S5: launch a new venture in a catalogued track; acquire or take over an existing operating cell; buy into an existing venture as an operating partner; or take a country or regional operating role.

**This stage is governed by** [`VENTURE-STAGE-GATES.md`](./VENTURE-STAGE-GATES.md), not by this document. The founder's personal record gates their *eligibility*; the venture's evidence gates the *venture*.

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** conflating "this person deserves an opportunity" with "this business should exist." Both must be true independently.

---

## S6 · Earn ownership

**Purpose:** convert verified contribution into a real, documented, legally valid interest.

Nothing vests without an executed instrument. The contribution ledger determines *how much* and *on what terms*; it does not itself create ownership. See [`OWNERSHIP-AND-EARNED-EQUITY.md`](./OWNERSHIP-AND-EARNED-EQUITY.md).

**Preconditions, all required:**
- Confirmed legal entity in the relevant jurisdiction
- Executed instrument between named parties
- Contribution ledger reconciled and countersigned by the participant
- Governance body decision recorded in the decision register
- Participant has received independent explanation of the terms in a language they read fluently

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** the single most common failure in ventures of this shape — informal equity promises that cannot be honoured, cannot be documented, and poison every relationship when the entity finally forms.

---

## S7 · Train the next cohort

**Purpose:** the ladder is only real if it reproduces.

Owners and senior operators carry a standing duty to develop successors. Instructor capability is itself certified — the ability to do the work does not imply the ability to teach it.

**Measured:** participants advanced under their instruction, credential pass rates on external assessment, curriculum contributions, and — the metric that matters most — **local trainers certifying unaided**, which is the corridor's core anti-extraction indicator.

**Failure mode this stage prevents:** a corridor permanently dependent on foreign trainers, which is the same dependency the vehicles were supposed to break.

## Cross-cutting rules

### Lateral movement
A participant may change venture track at any stage without losing credential or ledger history. Tracks are not castes.

### Holding and re-entry
A participant who stops — illness, caregiving, seasonal work, migration, a failed venture — retains their record and re-enters at the stage their evidence supports, not at the bottom. A failed venture is a recorded outcome, not a disqualification, provided the conduct record is clean.

### Demotion and removal
Removal from a stage requires: written finding, the participant's opportunity to respond, a named decision-maker, and a recorded rationale. Grounds are conduct, safety, fraud, or falsified evidence — never disagreement with leadership. Falsifying evidence is the one failure with no re-entry path, because the register is the asset.

### The equity firewall
No stage in this document creates equity. S6 is the only stage where ownership attaches, and only via an executed instrument. Any agent, document, portal surface, or person representing otherwise is in breach of the constitution and of `AGENTS.md` rule 3.

### Inclusion is designed, not hoped for
Where a stage's default structure excludes women, caregivers, participants without transport, or participants without a smartphone, the structure changes — schedule, location, childcare, device provision, offline-capable capture. Coverage gaps are tracked per cohort and published. See [`FIRST-COHORT-DESIGN.md`](./FIRST-COHORT-DESIGN.md) §4.
