# West African Mobility OS

An evidence-led operating system for building African-owned mobility, service, workforce, and entrepreneurial capacity - beginning with Mauritania and Senegal.

## Constitutional thesis

> This is not a Chinese vehicle dealership staffed by Africans. It is an African-owned commercial and entrepreneurial ecosystem that selectively uses Chinese products, technology, finance, training, and expertise to accelerate total local capacity.

Vehicles are the entry point. The durable system is the local network of entrepreneurs, technicians, workshops, parts operators, fleet managers, data stewards, financiers, and country builders created around them.

## Current phase

`Phase 0 - Supplier Discovery + Mauritania Evidence Sprint + MVP Validation`

The repository does **not** represent:

- A vehicle purchase order
- An OEM or distributor appointment
- Exclusive territory
- A government mandate
- Confirmed financing
- Verified fleet demand
- Permission to accept deposits
- A partnership, equity allocation, or joint venture

The active China-facing request is for proposals covering approximately **10 candidate vehicle models and their support packages**. It is not a commitment to purchase 10 vehicles.

## MVP purpose

The first MVP has two connected surfaces:

1. **Public market-validation portal** - captures structured, non-binding interest from individual buyers, fleet buyers, mechanics, apprentices, workshops, and service partners.
2. **Private intelligence dashboard** - allows authorized operators to review leads, supplier proposals, vehicle candidates, evidence, claims, decisions, tasks, service readiness, and landed-cost scenarios.

The MVP uses seeded demonstration data only. It must not take payments, accept deposits, send external messages, publish unsupported claims, or imply authorization.

## Initial geography

- **Mauritania:** controlled market-entry and evidence node
- **Senegal:** proposed complementary mobility, training, service, parts, and quality-assurance hub
- **West Africa:** long-term replication horizon, subject to country-level evidence and governance
