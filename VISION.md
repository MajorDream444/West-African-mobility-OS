# West African Mobility OS - Vision and Product Constitution

## 1. End-state vision

West African Mobility OS exists to help African countries own more of the economic system surrounding mobility.

The project is not designed merely to place imported Chinese vehicles into African markets. It is designed to use selected vehicles as the first practical demand engine for building:

- African-owned commercial enterprises
- Technician education and certification
- Workshop and diagnostic capacity
- Spare-parts and warranty operations
- Fleet uptime and institutional mobility
- Driver, mechanic, and entrepreneur ownership pathways
- Local data and operating intelligence
- Vehicle, workshop, and asset financing
- Women and youth participation
- Country-level venture incubation
- A replicable West African mobility corridor

Chinese manufacturers, technical specialists, products, diagnostics, training, and finance may accelerate the system. They must not become a substitute for African ownership, leadership, knowledge, enterprise, or data control.

## 2. The first strategic problem

Mauritanian consumers and institutions reportedly rely heavily on Japanese vehicles, particularly Toyota, because mechanics, parts, resale expectations, and market familiarity reduce ownership risk.

The working opportunity is not simply "sell cheaper cars." It is:

> Make additional vehicle choices credible by building the service, parts, training, warranty, financing, and information systems that protect vehicle uptime.

The market claims above remain reported until registration data, customs records, workshop mapping, buyer research, and commercial transactions verify them.

## 3. Initial country logic

### Mauritania

Mauritania is the first controlled market-development node. It can test:

- Affluent and family buyer interest
- Mining, logistics, contractor, government, bank, NGO, and institutional fleet demand
- Rugged SUV and 4x4 use cases
- New, hybrid, EV, and carefully governed used-vehicle demand
- Order-based sourcing with limited inventory
- Local workshop and technician readiness
- Landed-cost, warranty, parts, and resale assumptions

### Senegal

Senegal is a proposed complementary hub for:

- Taxi and managed-mobility demonstrations
- Technical training and train-the-trainer programs
- Existing Chinese-brand service knowledge
- Parts pooling and regional quality assurance
- Driver-to-owner pathways
- Women-led safe mobility
- Regional institutional coordination

Senegal is not represented as formally committed. Every partner, government program, training institution, dealership, bank, or fleet relationship requires documentation.

### Regional horizon

The long-term goal is a repeatable country-node model across West Africa. Each node must pass its own legal, demand, service, governance, and capital gates.

## 4. Business ecosystem

The operating architecture may eventually support several connected but separately governed businesses:

1. Vehicle discovery and controlled distribution
2. Institutional and mining fleet procurement
3. Managed taxi and mobility fleets
4. Certified used-vehicle inspection and commerce
5. Parts warehousing and replenishment
6. Workshop development and mobile servicing
7. Diagnostics, maintenance, and warranty administration
8. Technician education and certification
9. Roadside assistance and recovery
10. Charging and energy services
11. Vehicle, equipment, and inventory finance
12. Insurance and risk administration
13. Fleet software, telematics, payments, and performance reporting
14. Digital sales and community demand aggregation
15. Country venture incubation and ownership pathways

The first MVP must expose the opportunity without pretending that all these businesses already exist.

## 5. Entity and responsibility boundaries

### Marine Foundation

Appropriate lane:

- Education mandate
- Apprenticeships and workforce development
- Government and community convening
- Institutional relationship pathways
- Scholarships and inclusion
- Social-impact measurement

Prohibited default lane:

- Owning or financing vehicle inventory
- Accepting buyer deposits
- Acting as importer or dealer
- Guaranteeing commercial performance
- Carrying ordinary trading, credit, collision, warranty, or investment risk

### Commercial corridor venture

A future commercial parent may own the operating model, brand, intellectual property, country-replication playbook, standards, and strategic commercial relationships.

### Country operating companies

Separately governed country companies may handle local sales, import coordination, facilities, staff, service, parts, support, and fleet development.

### Ring-fenced asset SPVs

Separate SPVs may eventually hold taxi fleets, demonstration inventory, parts warehouses, charging assets, workshops, or training facilities so that unrelated risks do not contaminate each other.

### HAMAL

HAMAL is the proposed evidence, workflow, CRM, fleet, provenance, and operating-intelligence layer. HAMAL represents Hanzo, Art Mob AGInts, and Lux.

## 6. Local leadership and earned ownership

The system should build local leaders rather than route around them.

Diallo's proposed progression is:

`candidate -> envoy -> operator -> commercial leader -> partner -> builder and mentor`

Progression depends on verified contribution, reliability, risk-sharing, governance, revenue, customer outcomes, and the ability to train others. Introductions, ideas, local presence, or group-chat access do not automatically create equity, exclusivity, authority, or partnership.

The same ladder should be reusable for future country leaders.

## 7. Product architecture

### Public validation portal

The public product should help people:

- Discover approved candidate vehicles
- Compare use cases and indicative landed-cost scenarios
- Register non-binding personal buyer interest
- Request institutional or fleet information
- Join the automotive workforce
- Register workshop or service capability
- Understand how sourcing, service readiness, and future ordering may work
- See honest disclosures about what is verified, proposed, or still open

### Private operating dashboard

The private product should help authorized operators manage:

- People, organizations, roles, and relationships
- Suppliers and contacts
- Candidate vehicles and technical specifications
- Quotations, Incoterms, validity, and support packages
- Landed-cost scenarios
- Buyer and fleet qualification
- Mechanics, apprentices, workshops, and training cohorts
- Parts, diagnostics, warranties, and readiness
- Evidence, claims, conflicts, approvals, and historical truth
- Decisions, tasks, owners, deadlines, and next actions
- Reservations and orders only as controlled future states
- Fleet performance and maintenance as future modules

## 8. The MVP's controlled commercial loop

The first application should demonstrate one end-to-end loop:

`supplier proposal -> approved candidate vehicle -> public discovery -> structured interest -> operator qualification -> landed-cost review -> service-readiness review -> controlled readiness decision`

It should show what happens next without executing real payments, imports, contracts, messages, or reservations.

## 9. Evidence and publication controls

Every public vehicle, price, delivery estimate, warranty term, relationship, appointment, and authorization must trace to an approved evidence item.

The system should prevent publication when the relevant evidence is:

- Open
- Expired
- Contradicted
- Unsupported
- Unapproved

Required evidence classes:

- `VERIFIED` - supported by authoritative evidence
- `REPORTED` - stated by a participant
- `ASSUMPTION` - used transparently for planning
- `PROPOSAL` - a future structure under consideration
- `OPEN` - unresolved

Conflict resolution must preserve both historical truth and the current decision.

## 10. Safety and legal gates

The MVP must not:

- Take real payments or deposits
- Promise delivery
- Represent indicative prices as final landed prices
- Claim OEM, dealer, distributor, government, or territorial authority
- Treat interest as an order
- Imply Diallo or any participant has earned equity or exclusivity
- Merge Marine Foundation's nonprofit lane with commercial liability
- Solicit public investment
- Place automotive, gold, or smart-city opportunities into one transaction
- Send emails, WhatsApp messages, contracts, or supplier communications automatically

Before future deposits or binding orders, the program requires a confirmed entity, authority, import pathway, landed price, supplier authorization, customer contract, refund policy, payment custody, warranty, parts, service, insurance, and risk-allocation plan.

## 11. Design principles

The experience should feel:

- African-owned
- Institutional and trustworthy
- Commercially ambitious
- Modern and mobile-first
- Warm rather than bureaucratic
- Evidence-aware without overwhelming the public
- Multilingual-ready for English, French, and Arabic
- Appropriate for buyers, mechanics, institutional partners, and operators

Avoid charity imagery, foreign-savior framing, generic futuristic robots, fake government seals, invented partner logos, speculative wealth imagery, and claims that Africa is merely a labor pool.
