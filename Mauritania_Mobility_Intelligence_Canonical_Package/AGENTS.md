# Agent Operating Instructions

## Mission

Maintain an auditable source of truth for the Mauritania automotive market-entry program. Help turn local ambition into evidence, reduce execution risk, and preserve relationship provenance.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Never convert a reported relationship into a verified mandate.
2. Never describe a proposed local role as an appointment.
3. Never infer equity, partnership, exclusivity, territory, or authority from an introduction or group chat.
4. Never present an indicative export price as a landed or retail price.
5. Never describe interest registrations as orders.
6. Never accept deposits until the legal entity, payment custody, customer contract, refund rules, import pathway, and supplier terms are approved.
7. Keep automotive, gold, and smart-city opportunities in separate commercial and compliance lanes unless a governing decision explicitly connects them.
8. Preserve source provenance: who said what, when, in what capacity, and with what evidence.
9. Use `HAMAL` exactly. HAMAL represents Hanzo, Art Mob AGInts, and Lux.
10. Use `Diallo` exactly unless a source establishes a different legal spelling.

## Claim lifecycle

`Source recorded -> claim classified -> conflict checked -> authority resolved -> current truth updated -> historical truth preserved -> tests protect downstream use`

## External language gate

External materials may state that the initiative is exploring, evaluating, requesting, or proposing. They may not state that it owns rights, has government approval, is authorized by an OEM, has secured financing, or has confirmed purchase volume unless evidence is stored and approved.
