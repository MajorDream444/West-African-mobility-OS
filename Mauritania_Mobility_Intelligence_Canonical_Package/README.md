# Mauritania Mobility Intelligence

Canonical truth and market-development repository for evaluating a Mauritania automotive market-entry program connected to a wider West Africa mobility corridor.

## Current objective

Convert an early market hypothesis into evidence that can support supplier discussions, a controlled market test, local service readiness, and—only after proof—commercial appointments or investment.

## Current phase

`Phase 0 - Supplier Discovery + Mauritania Evidence Sprint`

No vehicle purchase, dealership appointment, distributorship, exclusivity, joint venture, government mandate, or financing commitment is represented by this repository.

## Canonical workstreams

1. China supplier EOI and proposal request
2. Mauritania field evidence sprint
3. Market and buyer research
4. Vehicle shortlist and landed-cost comparison
5. Service, parts, warranty, and training readiness
6. Public demand-validation portal
7. Internal HAMAL intelligence dashboard
8. Controlled pilot and evidence-based scale decision

## Repository map

## Evidence discipline

Every material statement must be labeled as one of:

- `VERIFIED` - supported by a named, reviewable source
- `REPORTED` - stated by a participant but not independently verified
- `ASSUMPTION` - used for planning and explicitly identified
- `PROPOSAL` - a future structure being considered
- `OPEN` - unresolved and assigned for validation

The repository preserves historical statements even when later evidence changes the current truth.
