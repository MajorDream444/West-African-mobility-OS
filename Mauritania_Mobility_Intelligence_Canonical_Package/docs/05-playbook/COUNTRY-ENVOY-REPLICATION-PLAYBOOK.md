# Country Envoy Replication Playbook v0.1

## Purpose

Create a repeatable method for identifying, coaching, testing, protecting, and advancing local market developers across Africa.

## Core doctrine

Local presence creates relevance. Relationships create access. Evidence reduces risk. Execution creates leverage. Sustained performance may create partnership.

## Stage 1 - Opportunity intake

Record the origin, people, geography, product, claimed demand, relationship provenance, and requested rights. No role or commercial promise is granted at intake.

## Stage 2 - Vision articulation

The envoy explains the opportunity, customers, local role, operating concept, and long-term ambition. This tests initiative and communication, not execution capacity.

## Stage 3 - Evidence sprint

The envoy completes a time-bounded market, legal, customer, facility, workforce, capital, and partner-validation sprint. Speed, honesty, organization, and evidence quality are assessed.

## Stage 4 - Controlled authority

The envoy may receive a written, limited mandate for specific meetings or activities. Authority has a territory, purpose, duration, reporting obligation, and revocation rule.

## Stage 5 - Pilot delivery

The envoy contributes to a controlled pilot with defined milestones, budget, risk allocation, and measurements.

## Stage 6 - Role and economics review

Titles, compensation, commissions, profit participation, equity, or expanded authority are negotiated based on assets contributed, risk carried, work performed, outcomes delivered, and replaceability.

## Stage 7 - Scale or exit

High performers advance into country leadership. Developing performers receive narrower assignments and coaching. Nonperformers lose authority without erasing their historical contribution.

## Protection principles

- Protect the local envoy from being bypassed when they create documented value.
- Protect the program from unsupported claims, premature rights, and relationship capture.
- Protect external partners from misrepresented authority.
- Preserve the origin and contribution record even when roles change.
