# Non-Binding Expression of Interest and Request for Manufacturer Proposals

**Mauritania Automotive Market Development Initiative**  
**Date:** 3 September 2026  
**Submitted through:** Brother Ling  
**Prepared by:** Major Dream Williams  
**Local market development contact:** Diallo, proposed and subject to final role documentation

## 1. Purpose

This non-binding Expression of Interest invites qualified Chinese automotive manufacturers, authorized exporters, and technical partners to propose vehicles and support packages suitable for a controlled market-entry program in Mauritania.

The initiative is evaluating how selected Chinese automotive brands can be introduced responsibly through market validation, limited initial inventory, customer preorders, local after-sales support, spare-parts readiness, technical training, and future institutional fleet development.

This document is a request for information and proposals. It is not a purchase order, dealership appointment, distribution agreement, exclusivity commitment, joint venture, government mandate, financing commitment, or guarantee of volume.

## 2. Market opportunity under evaluation

The initial market is Mauritania, with delivery expected primarily through Nouakchott and potential future service to other locations. Proposed customer segments include:

- Affluent individual and family buyers
- Companies, contractors, and industrial operators
- Mining and infrastructure companies
- Government institutions and public projects
- NGOs and international organizations
- Fleet operators and future dealers

The initial commercial model may combine a small number of demonstration or stocked vehicles with an order-based model in which qualified customers select vehicles digitally and order against confirmed commercial terms.

## 3. Requested candidate portfolio

Please recommend approximately 10 models across the following categories. Suppliers may propose a smaller portfolio when it produces a stronger fit.

- Premium-looking SUV and 4x4 vehicles
- Rugged vehicles suitable for desert, mining, construction, and institutional use
- Family vehicles
- Pickup, van, or light commercial vehicles
- Hybrid vehicles
- Fully electric demonstration candidates
- Selected new vehicles and, where lawfully supported, good-quality used or certified pre-owned vehicles

JETOUR T2 is one model of interest but is not the exclusive focus of this request.

## 4. Vehicle selection criteria

Proposals should address:

- Suitability for heat, dust, sand, rough roads, and long-distance operation
- Ground clearance, durability, and safety
- Fuel or energy requirements
- Availability of Arabic, French, and English interfaces or documentation
- Price advantage relative to established alternatives
- Design, comfort, technology, and perceived quality
- Warranty and claims administration
- Diagnostic-tool availability
- Critical spare-parts availability
- Technician training requirements
- Lead time and shipping readiness
- Expected maintenance cost and service intervals
- Battery thermal management, warranty, and replacement logistics for hybrid and EV models

## 5. Information requested for each model

Please provide:

1. Manufacturer, brand, model, trim, and model year
2. Vehicle category and recommended Mauritania use case
3. Powertrain, fuel grade or charging requirements, and key technical specifications
4. Export unit price and currency
5. Applicable Incoterm and named delivery point
6. Minimum order quantity and mixed-model order rules
7. Production or availability status
8. Estimated production, preparation, and shipment lead time
9. New, used, or certified pre-owned status
10. Warranty coverage, exclusions, term, and territorial applicability
11. Recommended initial spare-parts package and price
12. Required diagnostic equipment, software, subscriptions, and price
13. Maintenance schedule and technical-manual availability
14. Training options for an initial Mauritanian technical cohort
15. Remote support and escalation process
16. Marketing assets and permissions available
17. Dealer, representative, or distributor qualification requirements
18. Vehicle, inventory, or fleet-financing options, if any
19. Homologation or export documentation available
20. Any known restrictions affecting sale, software, warranty, or operation in Mauritania

## 6. Technical capacity and training request

The initiative is evaluating an initial train-the-trainer pathway for approximately 20 mechanics in Mauritania. Please propose one or more of the following:

- Chinese master technician deployed temporarily to Mauritania
- Lead technicians trained at a manufacturer facility in China
- Regional training delivered through an authorized West African facility
- Hybrid program combining regional or China-based training, local commissioning, multilingual digital materials, and remote support

The response should identify prerequisites, duration, curriculum, trainer qualifications, language support, equipment, assessment method, certification, cost, and continuing technical support.

## 7. After-sales and parts request

Please propose the minimum safe after-sales package for a controlled initial launch, including:

- Fast-moving and safety-critical parts
- Parts replenishment timelines
- Warranty-parts handling
- Diagnostic tools and software
- Technical manuals and service bulletins
- Remote escalation
- Initial workshop requirements
- Recommended staffing and technician competency

## 8. Proposed market-validation approach

Subject to supplier response and local validation, the initiative expects to:

1. Compare proposed models and full commercial terms.
2. Verify Mauritanian import, customs, registration, tax, insurance, and technical requirements.
3. Calculate landed and road-ready costs independently.
4. Publish only approved vehicles and accurate indicative terms through a demand-validation portal.
5. Collect qualified individual, fleet, workforce, and workshop interest.
6. Establish minimum service, parts, diagnostic, and warranty readiness.
7. Select a controlled demonstration or initial order based on evidence.
8. Evaluate longer-term representation or distribution only after performance is demonstrated.

## 9. Commercial and legal reservation

All quantities, roles, prices, timelines, territories, and commercial structures remain subject to due diligence, definitive written agreements, local legal and regulatory review, and approval by the relevant parties.

No recipient should rely on this EOI as evidence of an order, payment capacity, government approval, exclusive territory, distributor appointment, or guaranteed market demand.

## 10. Requested response

Please provide an initial response within 10 business days where practical, identifying:

- Responding legal entity and authority
- Proposed vehicle shortlist
- Commercial quotation validity period
- Technical and after-sales package
- Training proposal
- Required next steps
- Designated commercial and technical contacts

Following review, selected suppliers may be invited to a structured commercial and technical discussion.
